Fliptide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02 · com.tsorostudios.fliptide · Tsoro Studios (Tapiwa Makandigona), Kwekwe, Zimbabwe

Fliptide is a one-tap gravity-flip runner. It is single-player and has no account system, no login and no server of ours. This page says plainly what the game does and does not do with your data, for the web version and for the Android version.

The short version: Fliptide collects no personal data. The web version sends nothing anywhere. The Android version will show ads through Google AdMob unless you buy the one-time Supporter unlock, which removes them; the unlock is sold through Google Play Billing and we never see your payment details.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress. Best runs, attempt counts, the daily streak, unlocked skins and settings are stored only on your device — in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's local storage on the web. Deleting the app, or clearing site data, deletes them. Nothing is synced to us.
  • Course codes. A six-character course code is just a compressed description of a course. It contains nothing about you.
  • Sharing. The Share button builds a short text or a picture from your own run and hands it to the browser or to Android's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. We never receive it.

Web version (tapiwamakandigona.github.io and game portals)

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics. The web build contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no tracker. The only network requests it makes are for its own files from the host serving it.
  • Portals. When Fliptide is played inside a games portal (for example CrazyGames), that portal's own privacy policy governs the page around the game. The game itself still sends nothing.

Android version

  • Supporter unlock (in-app purchase). The optional one-time Supporter unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and handles refunds under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only an anonymous purchase token so the app can restore your unlock; we never see your name, card or address.
  • Advertising. The free Android version uses the Google AdMob SDK to show ads between runs. To serve and measure ads, AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data, under the Google advertising technologies policy.
    • Opt-out. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can decline personalised ads there.
    • Remove ads entirely. The Supporter unlock removes all ads; no ad SDK call is made once it is active.
    • Never inside a run, never between a death and the retry. Interstitials appear only at a session break — after you clear the course, or when you return to the app — never in the first minutes after install, and at most one every 3 minutes. The optional “second chance” video plays only when you tap the button asking for it.
  • No analytics beyond AdMob. We add no analytics SDK. The only measurement data that leaves the device is what AdMob itself needs to deliver and count ads.
  • Permissions. INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (ads and Play Billing), com.android.vending.BILLING (the Supporter unlock), and the permissions the AdMob SDK declares for itself: com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS (Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs), WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE (used by the SDK while an ad plays). No permission requires a runtime prompt. Optional in-game haptics use VIBRATE if present.

Children

Fliptide is aimed at players 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where a store or portal serves younger players, ads are requested with child-directed treatment as that platform requires.
