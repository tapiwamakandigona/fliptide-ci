# 0.5.0 — "Tidelight": a world outside the corridor, and a spark that notices

The first update of the October pass. Fliptide plays exactly as before —
same courses, same Daily, same codes — but it looks and reacts a lot more
like a living thing.

## What is new

- **A night coast above, a moonlit sea below.** On a phone in portrait the
  corridor is about a third of the screen, and the rest used to be two flat
  navy slabs. Now the ceiling carries a starry sky with a moon, two slow
  ridges and a dark lighthouse; the floor sits over a sea with gentle waves
  and a broken moon glint. Each layer scrolls at its own depth, so speed
  reads better. Floor and ceiling keep a solid edge, so the surfaces you run
  on still look like walls. On wide screens there is no room for it, so the
  corridor keeps just its solid edges and the night beyond them stays dark.
- **The spark glows.** It casts a warm light that also lights the walls
  around it.
- **The spark watches the danger.** When a spike, a pit or a wall is coming
  up on the surface it is running on, its eyes go wide, it stares at the
  hazard, stops blinking, and its mouth drops open at the last moment. Once
  the danger is behind it, it relaxes.
- **Near misses.** Flip away from a hazard at the very last moment and live,
  and the spark flares — the glow blooms and a little burst of sparks
  streams off behind it. Early flips don't count, and a row of spikes counts
  once.
- The character drawing reuses its paints instead of creating new ones every
  frame.

## Unchanged

Physics, courses, course codes, Daily seeds, The Tides, The Deep, ghosts,
saves and settings. Ads and the Supporter purchase work exactly as in 0.4.0
(Android only; web builds have no ads or purchases). No new permissions, no
new libraries, no new sounds.
