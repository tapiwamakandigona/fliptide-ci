# Join the Fliptide Android playtest

Fliptide is a one-tap gravity-flip runner from Tsoro Studios. Pick it if you
enjoy short precision runs; you do not need to be a developer.

> **Launch issue under investigation:** the owner reports that the Google Play
> version 0.3.0, and the previous version, crash before opening on an itel S26
> Ultra. The cause and a fix are not yet verified. We are pausing new Fliptide
> recruitment pushes while investigating. If affected, preserve app storage;
> do not uninstall or clear data just to try to fix it. [owner device/version report, 2026-09-09]

## Join in the right order

1. Join the [Tsoro tester group](https://groups.google.com/g/emberdelve).
2. Using the **same Google account**, open the
   [Fliptide closed-test opt-in page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.tsorostudios.fliptide).
3. Tap **Become a tester**, then follow **download it on Google Play**.
   The [store listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tsorostudios.fliptide)
   alone is not a substitute for opting in.

Group membership is not a game-test opt-in. Internal testing is a separate
track: Google says internal testers must first leave the internal test before
joining the closed test. Do not leave this Google Group to switch tracks.
If the store says the app is unavailable, check the selected Google account
and ask for help before uninstalling anything or deleting your save.

## A small, useful playtest

If you choose to participate, please remain opted in continuously for at
least 14 days and return for a few short sessions during that time.
You can leave whenever you wish.

- **First session:** Can you understand the first screen and the gravity flip?
  Try the opening courses. Tell us the first confusing or frustrating moment.
- **Return session:** Retry after a death, open a course code, and compare the
  daily course with a friend if you want. Does each control do what you expect?
- **Later session:** Close and reopen the app, check that progress is still
  present, and revisit the course that felt hardest.
- **Device/accessibility feedback:** Are buttons comfortable, text readable,
  and motion appropriate? Tell us about stalls, crashes or missing controls.
  Older/lower-end Android phones and larger text settings are valuable.

This suggested cadence is our feedback plan, not a Google requirement to open
the app every day. Google requires continuous opt-in and evaluates genuine
engagement and feedback when considering production access.

No purchase, ad click, public rating, positive review or screenshot proof is
required. Do not make purchases or click ads for this playtest.

on an announcement or the feedback email is preferable to trying to start a
